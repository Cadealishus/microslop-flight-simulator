# Vehicle and character models

These are converted from downloaded models by `tools/vehicle_import.py`. Each keeps its own licence (not the game's), and the converted files here are shared under the same licence as the original.

* **Ford F-150 Raptor R** (`raptor.bin.gz`): "2024 Ford F-150 Raptor R" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2024-ford-f-150-raptor-r-91aedf1119724f3396dcfe87686545c1) by Ddiaz Design (https://sketchfab.com/ddiaz-design), licensed under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Converted: re-scaled, wheels separated, textures baked into vertex colours.
* **Lamborghini Countach LPI 800-4** (`countach.bin.gz`): "Lamborghini Countach LPI 800-4" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lamborghini-countach-lpi-800-4-73af43bab25c41d88b80b88ee651b7c8) by VTX (https://sketchfab.com/VTX_car), licensed under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Converted: re-scaled, wheels separated, textures baked into vertex colours, repainted green.
* **The pilot** (`pilot.bin.gz`, `pilot.jpg`): "Nathan Animated 003 - Walking 3D Man" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nathan-animated-003-walking-3d-man-143a2b1ea5eb4385ae90a73657aca3bc) by Renderpeople (https://sketchfab.com/renderpeople), licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Converted: re-scaled, animation baked and walked on the spot.
